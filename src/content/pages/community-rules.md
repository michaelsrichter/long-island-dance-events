## Who can take part

- Anyone can **browse** this site without an account.
- You must be **13 or older** to like pages or leave notes.
- You must be **18 or older** to post photos.

## Notes

- Write about the dance, venue, teacher, band, DJ or style on that page.
- Do not post phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses or links. Organizers' public contact details are already on the page.
- Found a mistake in a listing? Choose **"A private correction for our editors"**. Only our editors see it.

## Photos

- Only post photos **you took**, or that you have the right to share.
- People who can be recognized must have **agreed** to be posted.
- **No photos where anyone under 18 can be recognized.** Many listings are kids' classes, so please check before you share.
- Describe the photo in a few words so people who use screen readers know what it shows.
- We remove hidden location data (GPS) and resize your photo. You still own it; you let us show it on this site.

## How we check posts

- An **AI checker** (Azure AI Content Safety) reads every note. Clear notes appear right away. Notes it is unsure about wait for a volunteer. Clearly harmful notes are not posted.
- **Every photo waits for a volunteer** before anyone else can see it.
- Notes with links, phone numbers or email addresses always wait for a volunteer.
- Anyone signed in can press **Report**. If three people report a post, it is hidden until a volunteer looks at it. Reports that a post shows a child, or shows you, hide it right away.

## If you break the rules

We may remove posts and pause or close accounts. Every decision is written down in our log. If you think we got it wrong, send a correction from any page and we will look again once.

## Take something down

- **A photo shows you?** Press **Report** and choose "It shows me". We hide it right away and remove it within 48 hours.
- **Copyright problem?** Use the takedown steps on the [Sources page](/sources/).
- **Delete your own posts and account** any time on [your account page](/account/).

This is a volunteer website and is offered as is.
