## Why this site exists

Long Island has a lot of dancing, but the listings are spread out. Some are in printed newsletters, some on studio websites, and some on social media. This site collects them so you can see your choices in one place.

## How we get the listings

A computer program reads public dance calendars once a week. It keeps only events in Nassau and Suffolk counties. It writes a short summary of each event in our own words and links back to the original. [Read how listings work](/sources/).

## Please check before you go

We do our best, but plans change. Before you drive somewhere, call the organizer or look at their website. Every event page shows how to reach them.

## Help us keep it right

If something is wrong or missing, use the "Report a problem" button on the event page, or [send us a correction](/sources/#corrections). Organizers can also ask us to remove their events.
